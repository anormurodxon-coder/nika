Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

NIKA ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel Handle (@Handle-k8h). It is used only by the channel owner. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube upload permission (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload): used only to upload videos that the channel owner has reviewed and approved to the owner's own channel.
  • OAuth tokens: stored only on the owner's personal computer. They are never sent to any third party.

The app does not read, collect, analyze or store any other Google user data, YouTube viewer data, comments, analytics or personal information.

How data is used and shared

Data is used solely to upload the owner's approved videos. No data is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties, and no data is used for advertising.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings. Locally stored tokens are deleted when access is revoked or the app is removed.
